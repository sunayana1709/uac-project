# Executive Summary — Care Transition Efficiency & Placement Outcomes

**Prepared for:** U.S. Department of Health and Human Services / policy stakeholders
**Scope:** 720 daily reports, Jan 12, 2023 to Dec 21, 2025

## Headline Numbers
- Transfer Efficiency Ratio: **0.691**
- Discharge Effectiveness Index: **0.024**
- Pipeline Throughput (period): **1.854**
- Backlog Accumulation Rate: **-5.73 children/day net**
- Outcome Stability Score: **0.439**

## What This Means
The care pipeline is trending toward backlog reduction,
with an average net change of -5.73 children/day across both CBP
custody and HHS care combined. The most severe sustained backlog window ran
**Jul 31, 2023 to Aug 17, 2023**.

## Recommended Actions
1. Direct case-management resources toward recurring backlog windows.
2. Address the weekday/weekend processing gap through staffing adjustments.
3. Adopt the Outcome Stability Score as a standing operational KPI, with threshold alerts.

*Full methodology and charts are in `research_paper.md` and the `figures/` folder.*
